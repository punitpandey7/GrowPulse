# Weekly Product Pulse — week ending 2026-10-20
*(Internal — Product & Support; source: 500 public reviews, 2026-08-02 → 2026-10-18)*

**Top themes:** Charges & fee transparency (116, avg 2.3★), Mutual fund & SIP experience (92, avg 2.1★), App stability & performance (15, avg 1.7★).

**What users are saying**
> “Why does Groww not show exit load before I hit redeem? Charged 150 rupees on a 6 day holding. Hidden fees vibes.” — Twitter, 1★, 2026-09-21
> “Redeemed my mutual fund for an emergency expense at 11 days and somewhere ₹487 vanished between shown and received. Support took days to even tell me it was exit load. Rest of the experience is genuinely smooth.” — Google Play, 2★, 2026-08-28
> “App crashed twice during market hours today. Lost a good entry on a limit order.” — Google Play, 1★, 2026-08-03

**Key observation:** Exit load on mutual fund redemption is the clearest recurring confusion: 52 of 121 fee mentions, 38 with explicit confusion signals. Users discover the charge only after redemption, then open tickets — it is a fund-house rule our flow never surfaces pre-confirmation.

**Actions for the product team:**
1. Show the scheme's exit load (amount + applicable %) on the redeem confirmation sheet before the user taps Confirm.
2. Surface the exit-load window and % on the fund detail page next to NAV, not only inside the scheme document.
3. Add a redemptions FAQ entry point in-app and route exit-load tickets to a macro with the new support snippet.
