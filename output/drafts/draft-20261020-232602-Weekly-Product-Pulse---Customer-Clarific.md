# Support snippet: why my mutual fund redemption amount was less than expected (exit load)
*Audience: Users asking: “I redeemed my mutual fund and got less money than the NAV x units. Where did the amount go?”*

1. Exit load is a fee payable to the mutual fund house for redeeming units before a specified period from the date of investment; it is set by the scheme, not by Groww. [Groww Help — What is exit load?]
2. Every scheme defines its own exit-load percentage and holding period in its scheme document — for example 1% if redeemed within 30 days, or 0.25% if redeemed within 7 days; many funds charge nothing after the period ends. [Groww Blog — Mutual Fund Fees and Charges in India]
3. The deduction is calculated as: exit-load % x redemption value, and the amount credited is the redemption value minus this load; STT may also apply for equity-oriented schemes. [Groww Help — What are the charges applicable for redeeming?]
4. It applies per redemption transaction, including partial redemptions, and only to units still within that scheme's exit-load window; units held beyond the window are not charged.
5. Groww shows an approximate exit-load amount on the redeem order screen before the order is placed, and the exact deduction appears in the redemption order details. [Groww Help — What is exit load?]
6. If a redemption amount looks lower than expected, users can check the scheme's exit-load terms on its Groww fund page or the help article, and contact support with the order ID for a breakup. Last checked: 2026-10-20. [Groww Glossary — Exit Load in Mutual Funds]
